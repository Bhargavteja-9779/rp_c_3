Overview

Progress in Artificial Intelligence is a comprehensive journal that publishes top-level research results in all fields of artificial intelligence.

Focuses on topics like data mining, soft computing, computational intelligence, and more.
Collaborates with the Spanish Association of Artificial Intelligence (AEPIA).
The Editorial Board comprises of leading figures in all related research topics.
Emphasizes on artificial vision, robotics, natural language, and Web intelligence.
