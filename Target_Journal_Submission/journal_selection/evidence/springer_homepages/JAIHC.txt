Overview

The Arabian Journal for Science and Engineering (AJSE) is a peer-reviewed journal owned by King Fahd University of Petroleum and Minerals (KFUPM) and published by Springer. Established in 1975, it publishes 24 issues annually and features high-quality research across a wide range of science and engineering disciplines. The journal is organized into two sections: AJSE-Engineering and AJSE-Science. AJSE-Engineering covers fields such as civil, chemical, electrical, mechanical, petroleum, computer science, and systems engineering, while AJSE-Science focuses on chemistry, earth sciences, physics, and biological sciences. Both sections publish full-length research articles, review papers on specialized topics, technical notes, and technical reports. AJSE provides an international platform for the dissemination of original research, supported by a rigorous peer-review process and strong ethical standards.
