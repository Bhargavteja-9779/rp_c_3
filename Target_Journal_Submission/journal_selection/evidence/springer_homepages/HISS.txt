Overview

Discover Applied Sciences is a multi-disciplinary open access journal covering applied life sciences, chemistry, earth and environmental sciences, engineering, materials science and physics.

Indexed in DOAJ, the Emerging Sources Citation Index (ESCI), Scopus with a CiteScore 2025 of 4.1, Ei Compendex and others.
Providing cutting-edge research to researchers, academicians, students, and engineers.
A journal in Springer Nature’s Discover Series: Rigorous, representative and wide-reaching.
