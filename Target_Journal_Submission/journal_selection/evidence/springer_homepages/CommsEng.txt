Overview

Discover Artificial Intelligence is an open access journal publishing research covering all aspects of artificial intelligence in theory and application.

Indexed in DOAJ, Ei Compendex and Scopus with a CiteScore 2024 of 6.0.
Covering industry, healthcare and medical diagnostics, transport, agriculture, and more.
A journal in Springer Nature’s Discover Series: Rigorous, representative and wide-reaching.
