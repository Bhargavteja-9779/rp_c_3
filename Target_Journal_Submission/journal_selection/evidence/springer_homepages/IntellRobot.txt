Announcement 

We are pleased to announce that Statistics and Computing will become a fully open access (OA) on 1 January 2027. As a result, all submissions received from 1 July 2026 are subject to an article publication charge (APC) if accepted and published in the journal (unless a waiver is applied). Please see our FAQs which can be found at the bottom of this page for more information on APCs, funding options, waivers and the journal's transition to fully open access.  

Statistics and Computing is a bi-monthly refereed journal publishing papers at the intersection of statistical and computing sciences.

Addresses the use of statistical concepts in computing science and the use of computers in data modelling, prediction and analysis.
Includes techniques for evaluating analytically intractable problems and big data analytics.
Publishes original research reports, authoritative review papers, and discussion papers.
Features special issues dedicated to emerging topics or proceedings of relevant conferences.
