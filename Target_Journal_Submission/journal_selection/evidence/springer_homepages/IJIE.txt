Overview

International Journal of Fuzzy Systems is an international, transdisciplinary journal focusing on the theory, design, and application of fuzzy systems, ranging from hardware to software.

Serves as a popular forum for researchers interested in fuzzy-related research.
Publishes regular papers, reviews, surveys, and expository essays.
