Overview

The Journal of Intelligent Manufacturing is peer-reviewed publication dedicated to the application of artificial intelligence in manufacturing.

Publishes research related to product development, manufacturing, and service systems.
Connects the research community with industry.
Features articles covering new methodologies and developments, case studies, and surveys.
Covers emerging areas including additive manufacturing, digital manufacturing, cyber-physical solutions, cloud applications, and deep learning.
Offers special issues on topics of interest to the readership.
