Overview

Artificial Intelligence Review is a fully open access journal publishing state-of-the-art research in artificial intelligence and cognitive science.

Publishes critical evaluations of applications, techniques, and algorithms in the field.
Provides a platform for researchers and application developers.
Presents refereed survey and tutorial articles.
Offers reviews and commentary on significant developments.
