Overview

The Journal of Ambient Intelligence and Humanized Computing (AIHC) explores AI systems that are human-aligned, context-aware and self-adaptive advancing research in AI-human collaboration and General Intelligence.

• Human-Centred AI & Trust – Ensuring fairness, transparency, and ethical AI-human interactions.
• Cognitive AI & Reasoning – Developing human-like decision-making and common sense in AI.
• AGI & Machine Consciousness – Advancing AGI frameworks and theories of artificial consciousness.
• Affective & Emotion-Aware AI – Enhancing AI’s ability to recognize and respond to human cognitive processes and abilities.
• Computational Efficiency & Adaptability – Designing biologically inspired, self-adaptive, and efficient AI models.
