LOOM submission package -- Journal of King Saud University - Computer and Information Sciences
==============================================================================================

Files and where they go in the submission system
------------------------------------------------
1_Main_Manuscript.pdf               Manuscript (anonymised, double-anonymous review). Upload as "Manuscript".
2_Supplementary_Material.pdf        Upload as "Supplementary Material" (referred to as Sect. S1-S6, Table S1-S9).
3_Cover_Letter.docx / .pdf          Upload as "Cover Letter".
4_Main_Manuscript_Word_version.docx Editable Word copy (equations are native Word equations; equation numbers
                                    are not carried over by the conversion -- use the PDF as reference).
5_LaTeX_Source.zip                  Standalone LaTeX source (Springer Nature sn-jnl template, sn-basic style):
                                    main.tex, supplement.tex, references.bib, tables/macros.tex (all numbers),
                                    tables/tab_*.tex, figures/*.pdf. Compile: pdflatex supplement; bibtex supplement;
                                    pdflatex main; bibtex main; then pdflatex supplement and main twice each.
                                    This was tested from a fresh extraction of the zip.
6_Title_Page.pdf / .docx            Upload as "Title Page" (contains author information; NOT anonymised).

What the authors must still do before submitting
------------------------------------------------
1. Fill in author names, affiliations, ORCIDs and the corresponding author (title page, cover letter).
2. Fill in funding, competing interests and author contributions (main manuscript "Declarations", title page).
3. Insert the code repository link (anonymised for review) in "Code availability" and the cover letter.
4. Read the whole manuscript and review the AI-use statements (Sect. 5.1 and Acknowledgements).
   All authors must approve the submission; the cover letter states this and must be true.
5. Date the cover letter.
6. Check the journal's current guide for authors (file types, article type, length) on the submission day.

Nothing in this package guarantees acceptance or a decision time.
