Medical & Biological Engineering & Computing serves the biomedical engineering community, covering the entire spectrum of biomedical and clinical engineering.

Official journal of the International Federation of Medical and Biological Engineering (IFMBE).
Founded in 1963 and expanded to 12 issues per year since January 2006.
Presents vital theoretical and experimental developments in biomedical science and technology.
Reports on advances in computer-based methodologies in multidisciplinary subjects.
High author satisfaction with 94% of surveyed authors expressing intent to publish again.
