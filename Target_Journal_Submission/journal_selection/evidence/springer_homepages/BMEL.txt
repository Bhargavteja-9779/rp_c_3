Overview

Neuroinformatics is a specialized journal that publishes original articles and reviews with an emphasis on data structure and software tools related to analysis, modeling, integration, and sharing in all areas of neuroscience research.

Covers theory and methodology, ontology, and various modeling approaches.
Publishes tests and evaluations of neuroscience databases and software tools.
Facilitates sharing of experimental results, massive data sets, and computational simulations.
Promotes neuroengineering approaches, including hardware, robotics, and information theory studies.
