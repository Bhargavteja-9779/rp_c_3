# Journal comparison (selection constraint: verified submission→acceptance ≤ 72 days, Q1/Q2 JCR, reputable publisher, scientific fit)

All acceptance times are **own measurements** of the median interval between the
`received` and `accepted` dates of research articles published in 2026 (Crossref
assertions for Springer Nature / Wiley / IET / T&F) or published July 2025 – Oct 2026
(PubMed history dates for OUP, Elsevier, IOP, PLOS, Frontiers). JIF values are the 2025
release as displayed on the publisher's journal page where it was scraped; "n/v" = not
verified in this study (no number is asserted).

Elsevier computer-science journals that are not indexed in PubMed (e.g. *Expert Systems
with Applications*, *Engineering Applications of AI*, *Knowledge-Based Systems*, *Results
in Engineering*, *Egyptian Informatics Journal*) could not be measured: Elsevier does not
deposit received/accepted dates in Crossref and ScienceDirect blocked automated access.
They were therefore **not eligible** under the "verified timeline" rule; this is a
limitation of the search, not evidence that they are slow.

IEEE journals (e.g. IEEE Access, IEEE JBHI) were likewise unmeasurable (no dates in
Crossref or PubMed); IEEE Access was additionally not assumed per the instructions.
MDPI journals were excluded by instruction.
