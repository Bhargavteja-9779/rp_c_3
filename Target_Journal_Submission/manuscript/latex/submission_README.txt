LOOM submission package -- Expert Systems with Applications (Elsevier)
=====================================================================

Files and where they go in the submission system
------------------------------------------------
1_Main_Manuscript.pdf               Manuscript (author details removed; elsarticle preprint layout). Upload as "Manuscript".
2_Supplementary_Material.pdf        Upload as "Supplementary Material" (referred to as Sect. S1-S7, Table S1-S10).
3_Cover_Letter.docx / .pdf          Upload as "Cover Letter".
4_Main_Manuscript_Word_version.docx Editable Word copy (equation numbers are not carried over by the conversion;
                                    use the PDF as reference).
5_LaTeX_Source.zip                  Standalone LaTeX source (Elsevier elsarticle class, elsarticle-harv style, both
                                    included): main.tex, supplement.tex, references.bib, tables/macros.tex (all numbers),
                                    tables/tab_*.tex, figures/*.pdf. Compile: pdflatex supplement; bibtex supplement;
                                    pdflatex main; bibtex main; then pdflatex supplement and main twice each.
                                    This was tested from a fresh extraction of the zip.
6_Title_Page.pdf / .docx            Upload as "Title Page" (contains author information).
7_Highlights.docx / .txt            Upload as "Highlights" (5 bullets, each at most 85 characters incl. spaces).

What the authors must still do before submitting
------------------------------------------------
1. Fill in author names, affiliations, ORCIDs and the corresponding author (title page, cover letter).
2. Fill in CRediT statement, funding and competing interests (manuscript back matter and title page).
3. Insert the code repository link in "Data availability" and the cover letter.
4. Read the whole manuscript and review the generative-AI declaration (manuscript back matter, title page,
   Sect. 5.1). All authors must approve the submission; the cover letter states this and must be true.
5. Date the cover letter.
6. Check the journal's current guide for authors on the submission day. It could not be retrieved automatically
   (the publisher's site refuses automated access), so the following were set from Elsevier's usual requirements and
   must be confirmed: highlights (3-5 bullets, max. 85 characters), abstract length (ours is under 250 words),
   name-year references, single- or double-anonymised review, graphical abstract (optional at most Elsevier journals;
   none is supplied), article type and length.
7. The paper was previously desk-rejected by another journal (JKSU-CIS) for limited novelty. Since then it has gained
   the PTB-XL study, the hybrid study and the post hoc realism analysis. Mentioning the earlier submission is
   optional.

Nothing in this package guarantees acceptance or a decision time.
