Overview

International Journal of Computational Intelligence Systems is an open access journal publishing research in all aspects of applied computational intelligence.

Emphasizes techniques like fuzzy logic, neural networks, evolutionary computation and probabilistic reasoning.
Affiliated with the European Society for Fuzzy Logic and Technologies (EUSFLAT).
Offers the widest possible readership and reach due to its fully open access nature.
Acceptance Rate in 2025: 13.5%
