Overview

Evolving Systems is a scientific journal focusing on continuous, adaptive machine learning and dynamically evolving AI systems.

Devoted to self-learning, self-developing, self-organized, and evolving AI systems.
Covers original, methodological, and application-oriented papers.
Encourages submissions related to machine learning, AI, computational intelligence and mathematical modelling.
Seeks inspiration from nature, including neuroscience, bioinformatics, and quantum physics.
Welcomes applications in engineering, health, environment, security, transport business, and social sciences.
