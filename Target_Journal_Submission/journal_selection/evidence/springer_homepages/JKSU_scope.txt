Aims and scope

The Journal of King Saud University Computer and Information Sciences is a refereed, international journal that covers all aspects of both foundations of computer and its practical applications. Special emphasis is given to recent trends related to the cutting-edge research within the domain.

The journal publishes original papers in the areas of, but not limited to:

1.     Artificial Intelligence

Fuzzy Systems
Machine Learning
Natural Language Processing
Data Mining

2.     Data Analysis and Representation

Big Data
Bioinformatics
