Overview

Complex & Intelligent Systems is an open access journal that focuses on the cross-fertilization of complex systems, computational simulation, and intelligent analytics and visualization.

Presents works on complex systems, computational simulation, and intelligent analytics.
Covers topics such as self-organizing collective systems, pattern recognition, and more.
Provides a platform for transdisciplinary research that addresses profound societal problems.
Committed to upholding the integrity of the scientific record as a member of COPE.
Offers a double-blind peer review process for all submissions.
Included in the ONOS sponsorship program, which supports authors from India (https://onos.gov.in/apc-support)
