Overview

Archives of Computational Methods in Engineering is a forum for disseminating the state of the art on research and advanced practice in computational engineering.

Publishes issues specialized on extended state-of-the-art reviews, a unique feature of the journal.
Emphasizes advances on computational mechanics, while examining computational engineering research and practices.
Offers a survey of the current literature and detailed critical expositions of recent research and applications in the field chosen.
Covers a variety of topics including modelling, solution techniques, and applications of computational methods.
