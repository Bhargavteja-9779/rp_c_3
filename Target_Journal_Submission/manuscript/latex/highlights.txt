Highlights
- A closed-form, leverage-aware confidence for fast random-kernel classifiers
- Pre-registered tests on 114 datasets and two real 12-lead ECG collections
- Its review queue caught 59 % of ECGs graded unusable; PreVal's caught 13 %
- Deep ensembles and MC dropout were less reliable judges of unusable ECGs
- No tested method flagged mild expert-annotated ECG noise much above chance
