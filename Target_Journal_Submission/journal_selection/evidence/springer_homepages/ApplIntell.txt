Overview

Applied Intelligence is a scholarly journal focused on research in artificial intelligence and neural networks. The journal presents new and original research and technological developments, addressing real and complex issues applicable to difficult problems. It provides a medium for exchanging scientific research and technological achievements accomplished by the international community.

Dedicated to addressing real-life problems that require unconventional approaches.
A platform for new and original research and technological developments.
