Overview

The Journal of Medical Systems is a leading Q1 journal in health informatics and medical systems, focused on how health information technologies are designed, implemented, evaluated, governed, and used in clinical and health care delivery settings. The journal publishes work on clinical decision support, electronic health record-driven analytics, mHealth and consumer health technologies, workflow engineering and simulation, imaging and signal processing, education and training, health policy, and the application, evaluation, safety, ethics, and governance of clinical and generative artificial intelligence.

The journal is organized into seven sections: Mobile Health; Implementation Science & Operations Management; Clinical Systems & Electronic Health Records; Image & Signal Processing; Consumer Health; Education & Training; and Health Policy.


The Journal does not consider pre-submission inquiries, nor does it accept unsolicited requests or submissions for special issues, topical collections, or Editorial Board positions.
